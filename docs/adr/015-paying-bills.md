# ADR-015: Paying bills: the inbox, payments that are expenses, and invoice details

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-11-10

**Context:** NM-21 (bills inbox with Due, Overdue and Paid), NM-22 (mark a bill paid) and NM-23 (enter the actual amount and an invoice reference) turn the bills of ADR-014 into something the user acts on. The spreadsheet marks a bill paid by typing in a cell and keeps the actual amounts in notes.

**Decision:**

- **Paying a bill creates a real expense.** The expense is in the bill's category, on the paid day, for the paid amount, with the bill's name (and invoice reference) as its note. The bill points at it (`bills.transaction_id`). A bill is paid exactly when it points at an expense. Why: spending totals, the dashboard and the insights already read expenses, so a paid bill shows up everywhere with no second place to keep in step.
- **The paid day and amount are the expense's own.** They are not copied onto the bill, so correcting the expense (Expenses screen) corrects what the bill shows. There is nothing that can disagree.
- **Paying is all or nothing and cannot double up.** The expense is created and the bill linked in one database transaction, and a bill can only be linked while it has no payment. Two taps at once, or two devices, give one payment and one expense (tested with six simultaneous requests against the real database). Paying several bills at once is one transaction too: if any of them is refused, none is paid.
- **The database protects the link.** A composite foreign key (expense, owner) means a bill can never point at another user's expense (ADR-006); a unique key means one expense cannot pay two bills; the expense of a paid bill cannot be deleted. Deleting it through the app answers 409 "Undo the payment on the Bills screen first." Deleting the whole user still removes everything.
- **Undo payment** opens the bill again and deletes its expense, in one transaction. Editing the expense stays allowed (its amount and day then show on the bill). The category of the created expense is the bill's; an archived category does not stop a payment, because the bill is already owed.
- **Statuses are worked out, not stored.** Paid has a payment; Overdue is unpaid with a due date before the day; Due is unpaid and due that day or later (a bill due today is not late yet). The rule lives in the finance engine (`billStatus`, `daysOverdue`). The server measures "the day" from `?asOf=` (the phone's own day, so evening in Skopje is not "tomorrow" on a UTC server); a day more than one day ahead is refused.
- **Inbox:** `GET /bills/inbox?tab=due|overdue|paid` returns one tab and the counts of all three. Due and Overdue are soonest due first (so the oldest overdue bill is first); Paid is latest payment first, at most 50 (the count is always complete).
- **Catching up (backfill), chosen by the product owner.** `POST /bills/sync` creates every bill that should exist and does not, from the month each obligation was **entered** (or its first month, if later) up to next month, at most 36 months back. So a month in which Bills was never opened still shows its unpaid bills as Overdue. History before the month of entry is not invented (an obligation with a first month of 2024 entered in 2026 does not create 2.5 years of overdue bills). The screen calls it once when it opens. `POST /bills/generate?month=` stays for a single month.
- **Invoice details (NM-23):** `PATCH /bills/:id` sets the actual amount and the invoice reference of an _unpaid_ bill; `null` takes either away. The actual amount replaces the expected one wherever the bill's amount is shown and is what a payment starts with; the expected amount stays on record. A paid bill's amount is the expense's, so a paid bill cannot be changed here (409). Amount of 1 to 100,000,000 denars; reference at most 60 characters, empty means none; the database checks both.
- **Bulk payment** pays the selected bills on one day, each for its own amount (no per-bill amounts: use the single form for that). At most 50 at once.

**Consequences:** the budget grid (NM-27) and "needs attention" (NM-30) read bills and statuses the same way. Loans and installments (NM-13 to NM-16) create bills that are paid the same way. A bill whose obligation was deleted needs NM-18 to decide (still not possible: obligations with bills cannot be deleted). A scheduler for reminders can call `sync` and `inbox` unchanged.

**Alternatives considered:** keep a `paid_on` and `paid_amount` on the bill and also create an expense (two copies that can disagree); a "paid" flag with no expense (every report would have to add bills to expenses); backfill from the first month of the obligation (can create years of overdue bills the user never owed in this app); allow deleting a paid bill's expense and mark the bill unpaid (a quiet side effect; refusing with a clear message is safer).
